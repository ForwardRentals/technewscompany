Squamish Water Taxi Adds Interactive Route Map, Fare Estimator and AI Assistant to Its Website

Visitors can now map a Howe Sound trip, see a rough fare instantly and ask questions any time of day.

Press release from Squamish Water TaxiOctober 19, 2026 · 2 min read

Photo: Squamish Water Taxi

SQUAMISH, British Columbia, October 19, 2026 —

Squamish Water Taxi, a boat service on Howe Sound in British Columbia, has added two tools to its website to make planning a trip on the water easier: an interactive route map with a fare estimator, and an AI assistant that answers questions around the clock.

Map a trip, see a fare

The new trip planner on the company's homepage shows Howe Sound destinations as pins on an interactive map. Visitors tap a destination, choose a starting point, set the number of passengers and pick one-way or round trip. The planner draws the approximate water route and shows the cruise time and a rough, distance-based fare estimate.

Estimates include up to three passengers, with additional passengers at $25 each up to six. The company notes that estimates are a guide, not a quote.

Questions answered any time

A chat assistant on every page of the site answers common questions about prices, pickup spots, tours and the Echo Falls trailhead shuttle, drawing only on the company's published information. When a visitor is ready to book, the assistant collects their trip details and passes them to the Squamish Water Taxi team to follow up.

Both tools are live now at squamishwatertaxi.com.

About Squamish Water Taxi

Squamish Water Taxi is a Squamish, British Columbia boat service offering water taxi trips, Howe Sound wildlife tours, trailhead shuttles across the Squamish River and boat rentals.
