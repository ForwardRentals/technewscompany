Tech News Company Launches Press Release Publishing for Startups and Technology Brands

Same-day publishing, permanent hosting and flat pricing from $149 per release.

Press release from Tech News CompanyOctober 8, 2026 · 2 min read
Tech News CompanyPress Release

SQUAMISH, British Columbia, October 8, 2026 —

Tech News Company today announced the launch of its press release publishing service for startups, software companies, hardware makers and technology agencies.

Companies can submit an announcement through TechNewsCompany.com and have it reviewed, formatted and published on a dedicated, permanent page, typically within one business day.

What's included

Every release receives:

  • A permanent, search-indexable page on TechNewsCompany.com
  • Professional formatting with headline, subheadline, dateline and boilerplate
  • Placement in the site's Press Releases feed and RSS feed
  • Structured data so search engines understand the announcement

Higher tiers add homepage placement, newsletter inclusion, rush turnaround and a professionally written release.

Clear labeling

All submitted releases are clearly labeled as press releases and kept separate from Tech News Company's independent news coverage. Outbound links in releases carry the industry-standard sponsored attribute.

Availability

Press release publishing is available now. Pricing and the submission form are at technewscompany.com/submit/.

Tech News Company covers the businesses building the future of technology, from AI and semiconductors to cloud infrastructure, security and consumer devices. It also publishes press releases for technology companies of every size.
