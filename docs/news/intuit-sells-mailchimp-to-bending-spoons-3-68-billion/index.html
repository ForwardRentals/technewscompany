Intuit to Sell Mailchimp to Bending Spoons for $3.68 Billion

The 2021 acquisition, bought for about $12 billion, will go to the Milan-based app buyer; closing is expected in early 2027.

By TNC NewsdeskNovember 19, 2026 · 2 min read
IntuitDeals & Funding

Bending Spoons said on November 19 that it has agreed to acquire Mailchimp from Intuit at an enterprise value of $3.68 billion. The Milan-based company will buy 100% of The Rocket Science Group, LLC, the entity behind the email marketing platform, along with other assets and liabilities of the business. Its board approved the deal unanimously.

Deal terms

  • Bending Spoons describes the transaction as all-cash. Intuit will reinvest $368 million of the proceeds in newly issued Bending Spoons equity.
  • Closing is expected in early 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and customary conditions.
  • Citi and J.P. Morgan are lead financial advisors to Bending Spoons.

Luca Ferrari, Bending Spoons' CEO and co-founder, said the company is "honored to welcome it" and excited about what the two can accomplish together. Jack Tam, a senior vice president at Intuit, said Intuit is focused on a smooth transition for Mailchimp's customers, partners and employees. Bending Spoons said it plans to invest substantially in the business after closing.

Price and rationale

According to a StockAnalysis report on the deal, Intuit paid about $12 billion for Mailchimp in 2021, so the sale price is roughly a third of that. The report said Mailchimp revenue fell 1% to $313 million in the quarter ended October 31, while Intuit's total revenue rose 12%, and that Intuit expects about $1.26 billion in Mailchimp revenue this fiscal year, flat to down 1%.

Intuit CEO Sasan Goodarzi was quoted by that outlet as saying that selling Mailchimp "sharpens our focus" on a strategy built around AI tools that help small businesses find customers and get paid. The report said Intuit plans to use the cash for share buybacks in fiscal 2027 after the deal closes, and that its guidance excludes the sale.

Context

Bending Spoons buys established digital products and takes over their operation. Its holdings include Evernote, Vimeo and AOL, and StockAnalysis, citing CNBC, said it bought Airtable in September. Mailchimp's own operating metrics were not disclosed in the Bending Spoons release.

Source: Bending Spoons (SEC Form 6-K)
