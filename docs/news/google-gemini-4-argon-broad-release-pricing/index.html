Google Opens Gemini 4 Argon to Developers and Subscribers, With $2 Introductory Pricing

The model is available through the API, the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search; Google says it ranks first on the Vals Index.

By TNC NewsdeskOctober 13, 2026 · 2 min read

Photo: David Nagle / CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

Google said on October 13 that Gemini 4 Argon is now available to developers, enterprises and eligible subscribers, with introductory API pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.

Where it is available

  • Developers and enterprises: globally via the API in AI Studio and the Gemini platform
  • Google AI Ultra subscribers: Google Antigravity
  • Google AI Ultra and Pro subscribers: the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search

Cached input tokens are discounted 95%. After November 30, 2026, pricing for the public model rises to $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens. The API offers a 1 million-token unified input and output limit.

Google's claims

The figures below come from Google's own post and have not been independently verified.

  • Ranked first on the Vals Index, which measures performance on finance, coding, legal and tax tasks
  • 87% on the ERQA embodied-reasoning benchmark
  • Attack success cut to 0.7% on the Gray Swan indirect prompt-injection benchmark across 15 adaptive attempts
  • Inside Google, agentic code submissions in Antigravity rose by more than 35% and code rollbacks fell by nearly a third

The post was written by Koray Kavukcuoglu, Google DeepMind's senior vice president and Google's chief AI architect.

Why it matters

The pricing step-up on November 30 means developers building on the model will face a doubling of per-token costs within about seven weeks. Benchmark results published by a model's developer are typically followed by third-party testing, which may differ.
