Funding Roundup: Vinci, Universal Quantum, Multiply Labs and Mecka Raise a Combined $485 Million

Chip simulation, trapped-ion quantum computers and robot training data drew big checks this week.

By TNC NewsdeskOctober 8, 2026 · 2 min read

Photo: IBM Research / CC BY 2.0 via Wikimedia Commons

Venture money continued to flow into AI-adjacent hardware and robotics this week. Here are the notable rounds.

Vinci: $250 million Series B

Vinci raised $250 million at a $1.5 billion valuation in a round co-led by Advent, Temasek and Xora, with AMD Ventures participating. The company started with AI-driven thermal simulation for semiconductor design. (Source: Vinci via Business Wire)

Universal Quantum: $100 million Series A

Universal Quantum, a 2018 spinout from the University of Sussex, raised $100 million co-led by DCVC and Firgun Ventures. Investors include Singapore's EDBI and Roblox co-founder David Baszucki. The company builds trapped-ion quantum computers using 200mm semiconductor manufacturing processes. (Source: Tech.eu)

Multiply Labs: $75 million Series B

San Francisco robotics startup Multiply Labs raised $75 million led by NantWorks, with AstraZeneca, Lux Capital and Founders Fund participating, bringing total funding past $100 million. Its systems are deployed with AstraZeneca and Legend Biotech. (Source: Business Insider)

Mecka: $60 million Series B

Mecka raised $60 million led by Sequoia, with Nvidia and Microsoft's M12 participating. Founded in 2024, it collects human movement data used to train humanoid and general-purpose robots.

Also notable

  • Manus raised more than $500 million after unwinding its proposed acquisition by Meta, according to The Information.
  • Vesta, a mortgage AI platform, raised a $30 million Series B led by Conversion Capital.
  • Turba Labs raised $52 million across seed and Series A for digital twins of computing infrastructure, the Wall Street Journal reported.
Source: Tech Startups daily roundups (Oct 6 and Oct 8)
