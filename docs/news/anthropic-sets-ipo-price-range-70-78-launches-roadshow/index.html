Anthropic Sets IPO Price Range at $70 to $78 a Share and Launches Roadshow

The AI developer plans to list Class A shares on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, alongside a $26 billion private placement.

By TNC NewsdeskNovember 10, 2026 · 2 min read

Photo: TechCrunch / CC BY 2.0 via Wikimedia Commons

Anthropic launched the marketing roadshow for its proposed initial public offering on November 10, setting a price range of $70 to $78 per share, RTTNews reported.

The offering

  • Shares: 801.35 million Class A shares from the company and 23.65 million from selling stockholders.
  • Option: underwriters may buy up to 123.75 million additional Class A shares.
  • Listing: Nasdaq Global Select Market, ticker "ANT".

The report did not name the underwriters or state a valuation.

Private placement

Alongside the IPO, AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron Technology, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners have agreed to buy an aggregate $26 billion of Class N common stock at the IPO price. Separate coverage says Alphabet and Nvidia are the largest buyers at $10 billion each. TSMC's board separately approved its portion on the same day.

Financial position

As of September 30, 2026, Anthropic reported having raised $141.1 billion in total capital, with $95.6 billion in cash and equivalents and $36.3 billion in short-term investments, according to the report.

Context

The final price is typically set after the roadshow once banks have collected investor orders, and a range can be revised through a prospectus amendment. Anthropic has already filed an amended S-1 in recent days that included a risk-factor warning about the pace of AI progress. Secondary outlets have offered various valuation estimates; since those are not from the company, they are not repeated here.

The final offer price and the first trading date had not been announced at the time of the report.
