Nvidia Reports $115.8 Billion Quarterly Revenue, Guides to $130 Billion as Vera Rubin Ramps

In its first earnings report since Jensen Huang's resignation, interim CEO Colette Kress said the Vera Rubin NVL72 is the fastest product ramp in company history.

By TNC NewsdeskNovember 17, 2026 · 2 min read

Photo: Will Buckner / CC BY 2.0 via Wikimedia Commons

Nvidia reported revenue of $115.8 billion for its third quarter of fiscal 2027, which ended October 25, up 20% from the previous quarter and 103% from a year earlier. The results, released on November 17, were the first since co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive last week; Colette Kress, who is both interim CEO and chief financial officer, led the announcement.

The numbers

  • Data Center revenue: $107.6 billion, up 21% from the prior quarter and 110% from a year ago.
  • Edge Computing revenue: $8.2 billion, up 14% sequentially and 42% year over year.
  • Earnings per share: $2.70 on a GAAP basis and $2.68 non-GAAP.
  • Net income: $65.3 billion GAAP and $64.9 billion non-GAAP.
  • Gross margin: 74.8% GAAP and 74.9% non-GAAP.

Outlook and capital returns

For the fourth quarter, which includes an extra week, Nvidia guided to revenue of $130.0 billion, plus or minus 3%, and said the forecast assumes no Data Center compute revenue from China. It expects gross margins of 71% to 72%.

Nvidia returned $28.1 billion to shareholders through buybacks and dividends during the quarter. About $227.3 billion remained under its repurchase authorization, which the board increased by $150 billion in September. The next quarterly dividend is $0.25 per share, payable December 23 to holders of record on December 2.

Vera Rubin and leadership

Nvidia said Vera Rubin NVL72 racks have shipped and that the ramp is the fastest in its history. It named CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and Nebius as operators running the racks, with SpaceXSI coming online soon.

"Vera Rubin is off to an explosive start, our roadmap is on track and our ambition has never been greater," Kress said in the release.

The release also noted that the company is searching for a new CEO and that an independent board committee is conducting an investigation. Earlier coverage on this site detailed [Huang's resignation](/news/nvidia-jensen-huang-resigns-colette-kress-interim-ceo/) and the [board's independent review](/news/nvidia-board-independent-review-jensen-huang-megaspeed-spy-report/).

Context

Revenue of this size reflects continued spending on AI data centers by cloud providers and AI developers. The guidance excluding China compute revenue is consistent with export restrictions that have limited Nvidia's sales there. The company did not name a timetable for choosing a permanent chief executive.
