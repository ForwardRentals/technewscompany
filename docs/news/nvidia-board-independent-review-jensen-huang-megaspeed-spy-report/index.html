Nvidia Board Opens Independent Review After Report Chinese Spy Operation Targeted Jensen Huang

Huang told employees he was unfaithful to his wife; the board says it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised.

By TNC NewsdeskNovember 8, 2026 · 2 min read

Photo: Anderseidesvik / CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

Nvidia's board of directors said on Sunday it has launched an independent investigation into reports that CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a Chinese intelligence operation, TechCrunch reported.

What the board said

Independent directors will run the review with outside legal counsel. The board said it has "no reason to believe" national security or company secrets were compromised, and that it has confidence in Huang and his leadership team. According to the board, Huang supports the review and has committed to cooperating.

Huang's message to employees

Nvidia also published a memo from Huang to staff in which he acknowledged an affair without naming the woman involved. "I was unfaithful to my wife," he wrote, adding that he had fallen short of his responsibility to the company and apologizing to his family and employees.

TechCrunch identified the woman as Alice Huang, a former executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based chip company.

The underlying report

The review follows a TechCrunch report on November 7 about a memo circulating among U.S. policymakers earlier this year. The memo claimed that China's Ministry of State Security recruited an executive to spy on Nvidia's chief executive. Before the board's statement, Nvidia had said the pair's interactions were "not unusual" and that it does not share classified information with customers.

  • Review led by: independent directors and outside counsel
  • Board position: no indication of compromised secrets or national security
  • Trigger: a U.S. policy memo alleging a recruitment operation

Why it matters

Nvidia's chips sit at the center of the U.S.-China competition over AI, and Huang is among the most prominent figures in the industry, with a close relationship with President Donald Trump, TechCrunch noted. The claims in the memo are allegations and have not been independently established; the board's review is the first formal step Nvidia has announced. No timeline for the review was reported.
