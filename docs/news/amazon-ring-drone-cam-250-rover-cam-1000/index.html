Amazon's Ring Launches $250 Drone Cam and $1,000 Rover Cam Home-Security Robots

The Drone Cam goes on sale in the US in late January 2027, according to Silicon UK.

By TNC NewsdeskNovember 11, 2026 · 2 min read
AmazonDevices

Amazon's Ring doorbell brand has unveiled a home-surveillance drone, the Ring Drone Cam, priced at $250 and due to go on sale to US customers in late January 2027, according to Silicon UK on November 11, 2026. It also introduced the Ring Rover Cam, a $1,000 patrol robot.

The details

  • Drone Cam: Does most of its processing in the cloud, so it needs Wi-Fi for everything except takeoff and landing. Users can set a patrol route or have it investigate when a Ring doorbell or camera detects motion.
  • Flight time is about eight minutes, after which it returns to its dock to recharge.
  • While flying it makes a loud buzzing sound and shows LED lights, intended to deter intruders.
  • Rover Cam: Built from Amazon's discontinued Astro robot and marketed to businesses for night patrols. A light projects onto the ceiling as it moves so users can track its position.

Background

Ring showed an earlier indoor drone, the Always Home Cam, in 2020. Ring CEO Jamie Siminoff has said the company struggled to balance on-board sensors, weight and battery life; moving processing to the cloud was the design shift that made the new model workable, according to the report.

Why it matters

The products extend Ring from fixed cameras into mobile, autonomous devices, a category that has drawn privacy and surveillance debate. Only a single outlet's account was available when this article was written; pricing and availability should be confirmed with Amazon before purchase.
