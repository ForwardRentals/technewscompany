Google Gives Gemini on Mac the Ability to Operate Apps and Browse on a User's Behalf

The new computer-use feature launches in English in the U.S. for Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers, with controls to pause, stop and approve actions.

By TNC NewsdeskNovember 19, 2026 · 2 min read

Photo: Asoundd / CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

Google said on November 19 that its Gemini desktop app for Mac can now use a computer on a person's behalf, working in native Mac apps and browsing websites in the background, TechCrunch reported.

What it does

The feature handles multistep tasks across local files, apps and the web. Google's suggested uses include:

  • Filling out forms
  • Repeating a task across a batch of files
  • Sorting files into folders by given criteria
  • Building slideshows from files or from data on online dashboards

Gemini shows its progress in a live preview inside the chat, and the user's screen and cursor remain free for other work. Users can pause, stop, and approve actions before the agent takes them.

Availability

The launch is limited to English in the United States. It requires the Gemini app for Mac and a Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra subscription. Google did not say when it would reach other countries or platforms, and TechCrunch's report did not give subscription prices.

Context

TechCrunch noted that Google is arriving after OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta, which already offer agentic features in their desktop apps. The release follows Google's rollout of Gemini "Tasks" earlier in the week, which can help sign in to websites, make purchases and book appointments.

Agents that control a desktop raise permission and security questions, and Apple has been adding controls over how AI agents access files in macOS, as covered on this site. Google's approval step before actions are taken is one safeguard in the design. The company has not published detail on how the agent handles sensitive data on a user's machine.

The feature is part of a broader move by large AI developers from chat assistants toward agents that complete work directly in the apps people already use.
