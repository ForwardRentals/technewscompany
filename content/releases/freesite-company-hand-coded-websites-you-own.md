Small businesses usually face two choices for a website: pay an agency thousands of dollars up front, or spend their own time on a drag-and-drop builder they can never fully leave. FreeSite Company, a web design studio in Squamish, British Columbia, offers a third option: a custom, hand-coded website, built for free.

## Built by hand, owned by the client

FreeSite Company builds every site from scratch rather than from a template. Each build includes:

- **Hand-coded pages** that load fast on phones
- **Local SEO foundations**: schema markup, meta tags and an XML sitemap
- **Contact, quote or booking forms**
- **Analytics and Meta pixel setup**
- **Two full rounds of revisions**
- **Full source code** handed to the owner

Unlike hosted builders, which typically keep a site tied to their platform, FreeSite Company clients receive their code and can host it anywhere or hand it to another developer.

## Pricing

The build is free, and owners see the finished site live before paying anything. Hosting and support cost $9.99 a month if they choose to keep it, with no contract.

The company also published a new comparison of Wix, Squarespace and custom websites on its blog, covering ownership, speed, cost and when each option makes sense.

Applications are open at freesitecompany.com.

### About FreeSite Company

FreeSite Company is a Squamish, British Columbia web design studio that builds custom, hand-coded websites for small businesses across Canada for free, with optional hosting and support from $9.99 a month.
