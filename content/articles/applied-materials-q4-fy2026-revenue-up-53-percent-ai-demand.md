Applied Materials said on Thursday that revenue for its fiscal fourth quarter rose 53% from a year earlier to $10.41 billion, above analyst expectations of about $10.3 billion, SiliconANGLE reported.

## The numbers

- **Adjusted earnings per share:** $4.16, versus an estimate of $4.06.
- **Net income:** $2.71 billion, up from $1.89 billion a year earlier.
- **Semiconductor Systems revenue:** $7.99 billion, up from $4.88 billion.
- **Applied Global Services revenue:** $1.89 billion, up from $1.5 billion.
- **Fiscal 2026 sales:** $34.31 billion, up 21%, which CEO Gary Dickerson described as a record.

The company expects its Semiconductor Systems business to grow more than 40% in calendar 2026.

## Outlook

For the next quarter Applied guided to revenue of about $11.1 billion and adjusted EPS of $4.35 at the midpoint. Analysts had expected roughly $10.85 billion and $4.24. Dickerson said AI "continues to fuel strong incremental demand for semiconductors."

## EPIC Center investment

Applied also said it plans to invest $5 billion in its EPIC Center, a shared research and development facility in Silicon Valley. Initial partners include Intel, Kioxia, BE Semiconductor Industries and UCLA Samueli.

## Market reaction

Shares fell earlier in the day, which SiliconANGLE linked to news of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation, then rose more than 2.5% in late trading. The stock is up about 48% so far this year, according to the report.

Equipment makers sit upstream of chipmakers such as TSMC and Intel, so their results are watched as a signal of planned manufacturing capacity. Related: [Nvidia's CEO change](/news/nvidia-jensen-huang-resigns-colette-kress-interim-ceo/).
