Anthropic is buying about $3.4 billion worth of Caterpillar natural gas generators, according to the Financial Times as relayed by TechCrunch on November 9. The equipment could supply up to 1.7 gigawatts of electricity.

## The deal

The generators were originally ordered by OnePWR, an equipment supplier, a couple of years ago. OnePWR could not find financing for the order, TechCrunch reported, without saying why. Anthropic had planned to buy from OnePWR but went directly to Caterpillar instead.

Anthropic has not been known for owning infrastructure. In its May S-1 filing it said it pays SpaceX subsidiary xAI $1.25 billion per month through May 2029 for computing power, and that it expects "to enter into additional similar services contracts."

## Why it matters

Gas turbine order backlogs run into the early 2030s, and grid connection queues can be just as long, TechCrunch noted. The outlet described the purchase as a last resort and suggested Anthropic may also have wanted to keep the capacity from competitors; that is TechCrunch's interpretation, not a statement from the company.

## Context

- Google, Amazon, Microsoft and Meta have each announced plans for on-site natural gas power at data centers.
- The buildout has revived fracking, TechCrunch said.
- U.S. data centers could use more natural gas than Germany and Japan combined by 2035, the outlet reported.
- One analyst expects gas prices to more than triple in some markets by the end of the decade.

Anthropic, which plans to go public this year, had not been reported to comment on the purchase in the TechCrunch article.
