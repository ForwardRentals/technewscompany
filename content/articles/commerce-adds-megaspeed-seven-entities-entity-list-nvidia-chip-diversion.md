The U.S. Commerce Department on Monday, November 23, added Singapore-based Megaspeed and six other entities to its Entity List, restricting exports of advanced technology to them, according to Semafor.

## What the listing does

Under the listing, U.S. companies need a government license to sell certain technologies, including semiconductors, to Megaspeed or its foreign affiliates. Semafor reported that licenses will be reviewed under a presumption of denial. The filing cites a "significant risk of these entities being or becoming involved in the diversion of advanced computing commodities."

- **Entities:** seven, located in Singapore, Malaysia and Thailand
- **Named affiliates:** MSFusion, Speedmatrix, Apex Enterprise Solutions and Swiftdata
- **Shipments already en route:** appear to be allowed to proceed
- **Reach:** a Bureau of Industry and Security spokesman said exporting advanced chips to Megaspeed is now unlawful even when they are produced outside the United States

## Context

Megaspeed is the cloud company at the center of the controversy involving Nvidia and its chief executive. The Justice Department is investigating whether Megaspeed violated U.S. export controls by shipping Nvidia chips to China. CNBC reported that the Justice and Commerce departments jointly announced a probe on November 17.

Semafor reported that Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick faced pressure from elsewhere in the administration and from bipartisan lawmakers over the decision. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Treasury, the Pentagon, the State Department and the U.S. Trade Representative had backed the move "for months," adding that "Commerce holds the pen."

## Same-day Nvidia news

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO the same day, after he resigned on November 12. Reporting on the two events has linked them, but the Entity List filing itself concerns only the listed companies. Bloomberg separately reported on Tuesday that the U.S. blacklisted an Alibaba-owned company; Semafor lists Apex Enterprise Solutions among the Megaspeed affiliates, and the relationship between those accounts has not been reconciled here.

Nvidia has not been accused of wrongdoing in the listing itself.
