OpenAI's operating loss widened to $10.9 billion in the third quarter even as revenue grew about 50%, according to the Wall Street Journal, as summarized by Silicon UK on November 12. The figures come from documents the Journal reviewed; OpenAI has not published them.

## The numbers

- **Operating loss:** $10.9 billion, up from $10.1 billion in the previous quarter.
- **Revenue:** About $10 billion for the quarter, an increase of roughly 50%.
- **Adjusted operating margin:** Negative 109%, improved from negative 150% the quarter before.

In other words, the company spent more than twice what it earned in the period, though the gap narrowed relative to revenue.

## Comparison with Anthropic

Silicon UK placed the report alongside figures from Anthropic's pre-IPO S-1 filing. Anthropic's annualized revenue run-rate reached $81 billion in October, $10 billion higher than the month before, the filing shows. For the six months to the end of June, revenue was $16.2 billion, up from $1.1 billion a year earlier, while the pre-tax loss rose to $80.7 billion from $12.9 billion. Expenses grew faster than revenue, according to the filing.

Silicon UK reported that Anthropic's IPO is expected next week and is believed to value the company at about $1.4 trillion; that valuation has not been confirmed by the company.

## Context

Both companies are spending heavily on computing capacity and model training. Investors and regulators are watching how quickly revenue can catch up with those costs. OpenAI's executives have separately said the company could be ready for an IPO in 2027, a timeline covered in earlier reports.
