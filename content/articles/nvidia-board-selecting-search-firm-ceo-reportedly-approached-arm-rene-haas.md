Nvidia's board said on November 13 that it is still choosing a search firm to find a permanent chief executive and has not formally engaged any candidates, according to CNBC. The statement came a day after the company announced that Jensen Huang would step down as CEO.

## What the board said

The board told CNBC it is "in the process of selecting a search firm for the Nvidia CEO role" and that it has "not formally engaged any potential candidates." It called speculation about individual names "premature." Nvidia will consider both internal and external candidates.

CFO Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO. CNBC, citing a person familiar with the matter, reported she held her first meeting with senior executives at the company's Santa Clara, California, headquarters on Thursday afternoon.

- **Reported candidate:** The Financial Times reported on Friday, citing people familiar with the matter, that the board approached Arm CEO Rene Haas. Haas worked at Nvidia for seven years before joining Arm in 2013. Nvidia has not confirmed the report.
- **Share price:** Nvidia stock fell more than 2% on Friday, according to CNBC, leaving its market value below $4 trillion.

## Investigation and Washington scrutiny

On November 10, Nvidia retained the law firm Wachtell Lipton to investigate allegations that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. The allegations followed reports about his relationship with a woman several outlets say is suspected of working for Chinese intelligence.

CNBC reports that three House committees asked Huang to appear for briefings and renewed those requests after his resignation. The Senate Banking Committee also asked Huang and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick to testify about what it described as "serious concerns about our national security" and about how Nvidia's top AI chips reportedly reached China.

## Context

Huang co-founded Nvidia and led it through its rise to become one of the world's most valuable companies. Choosing a successor will shape strategy at a company central to AI data-center spending, and the process is unfolding alongside the legal review and congressional requests. Nvidia has not given a timeline for naming a permanent CEO.
