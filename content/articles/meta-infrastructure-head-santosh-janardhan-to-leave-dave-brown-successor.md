Meta said its longtime head of infrastructure, Santosh Janardhan, will leave the company next year, with Dave Brown set to replace him, according to a Silicon UK report on November 12 that cites Meta and Bloomberg.

## Who is involved

- **Santosh Janardhan:** Joined Meta in 2009 and is also co-head of engineering. He will stay through a transition period, and Bloomberg reports he is expected to remain through the first quarter.
- **Dave Brown:** Joined Meta earlier this year as vice president of infrastructure. According to his LinkedIn profile, he previously spent 19 years at Amazon Web Services, working on compute, cloud and AI.

In a memo to staff reported by Bloomberg, Janardhan gave no reason for leaving.

## Why it matters

Infrastructure is among the largest cost centers at Meta. Like other major technology companies, it is greatly increasing its data-center investment to build the computing power needed for AI services. Leadership of the teams that build and run those facilities therefore carries significant weight in the company's spending plans.

## Context

Meta has recently changed other senior roles, including the departure of chief strategy officer Dave Wehner, covered earlier on this site. The company has not said whether Brown's remit will change when he takes the lead role, and it did not announce a departure date for Janardhan beyond "next year."
