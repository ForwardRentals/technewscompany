Anthropic announced on November 18 that it has priced its initial public offering at $83.00 per share, above the $70 to $78 range it set when the roadshow began on November 10. The Class A common stock is listed on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol ANT, and trading was scheduled to start on November 19.

## The offering

According to Anthropic's announcement, the deal covers 980,000,000 Class A shares:

- 956,351,354 shares are being sold by Anthropic.
- 23,648,646 shares are being sold by certain selling stockholders.
- Underwriters hold a 30-day option to buy up to 147,000,000 additional shares.
- The offering is expected to close on November 20, subject to customary closing conditions.

The company said the SEC declared its registration statement effective on November 18. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are named as sources for copies of the final prospectus. Anthropic did not state gross proceeds in the announcement; multiplying the share count by the price implies roughly $81 billion in total, a back-of-the-envelope figure rather than a reported one.

## Private placement and valuation

Alongside the public offering, Anthropic previously disclosed a $26 billion private placement of non-voting stock to strategic partners. Media outlets, including Fortune, reported a valuation of about $1.5 trillion at the IPO price; other outlets calculate a lower figure of roughly $1.08 trillion, depending on whether options and reserved employee shares are counted. Anthropic's announcement does not give a valuation.

## Context

The listing follows the company's S-1 filing and a series of amendments over recent months, and makes Anthropic one of the first frontier AI developers to become publicly traded. The first-day opening price is set by buy and sell orders on the exchange and can differ from the IPO price. As of this writing, a first-day close had not been confirmed in sources reviewed.
