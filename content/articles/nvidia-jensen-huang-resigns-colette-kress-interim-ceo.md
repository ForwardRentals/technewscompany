Nvidia said on Thursday that co-founder Jensen Huang has resigned as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately, ending a 33-year tenure. Chief financial officer Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO while the board looks for a permanent successor, TechCrunch reported.

## What led to the departure

The announcement followed several days of reporting about Huang's personal relationships, including an alleged relationship with a person described in reports as a Chinese spy and another with a direct report at Nvidia. Those accounts are allegations in press reports; this article does not treat them as established findings. The board had earlier opened an independent review of the matter and was reported to be weighing Huang's removal.

According to TechCrunch, Nvidia had no succession plan in place. Nvidia is valued at roughly $3.8 trillion, per the same report.

## Who is Colette Kress

- Age 59; joined Nvidia in September 2013 and has been CFO since.
- Previously spent three years as senior vice president and CFO of Cisco's business technology operations finance organization.
- Spent 13 years at Microsoft, including four as CFO of its Server and Tools division.
- Holds a finance degree from the University of Arizona and an MBA from Southern Methodist University.

Kress said she is "committed to building on the extraordinary foundation Jensen created over the last 33 years," and that the company's focus remains on customers, partners, developers and shareholders.

## What to watch

Nvidia is scheduled to report quarterly earnings on Tuesday, when Kress is expected to outline near-term plans. The search for a permanent CEO is open, and the company has not said how long it expects the process to take.
