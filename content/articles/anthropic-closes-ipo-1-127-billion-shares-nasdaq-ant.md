Anthropic said on November 20 that it has closed its initial public offering, selling 1,127,000,000 Class A shares at $83.00 each after underwriters fully exercised their option to buy 147,000,000 additional shares. A concurrent private placement also closed, according to the company's announcement.

## The numbers

Anthropic's own announcement lists the share count and price. Proceeds figures come from a Fox News live blog, which put total gross proceeds at $119.5 billion before fees and expenses:

- $81.3 billion from the base Class A offering
- $12.2 billion from the underwriters' additional shares
- $26 billion from the private placement of non-voting Class N stock

The company had priced the deal on November 18 above its $70 to $78 range. Shares trade on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol ANT.

## First two sessions

Trading began Thursday, November 19. According to Fox News, the stock opened at $111.50, reached an intraday high of $115.10 and closed at $97.17, about 17% above the offering price. AP and Forbes also reported the $97.17 close. Fox said volume topped 851 million shares.

On Friday the shares opened at $89.86, down about 7.5% from Thursday's close, and traded in the high $80s later in the day, still above the IPO price, according to the same live blog. Valuation estimates differ by source; Fox Business cited a fully diluted figure of about $1.5 trillion, while other coverage cited about $1.3 trillion based on closing prices.

## Context

Anthropic's S-1 disclosed large infrastructure obligations. Fox News, summarizing the filing, reported about $70.7 billion in compute and lease commitments due in the 12 months through September 30, 2027, and a $125.2 billion five-year lease with Broadcom for specialized AI chips. It also cited pro forma cash of roughly $232 billion.

Fox Business noted that up to 315 million shares held by non-founder insiders and employees could become eligible for sale on the first day, subject to lock-up limits.

The listing follows the company's earlier S-1 filing and roadshow, which began November 10. Share prices move constantly; this article is news reporting and not investment advice.
